# EPK (Electronic Press Kit) Structure

## Required Sections
1. **Cover** — artist photo + name + one-line positioning
2. **Bio** — three lengths:
   - Short (50–75 words) — for playlists/social
   - Medium (150–200 words) — for press/venues
   - Long (300–500 words) — for full press kit
3. **Music** — 2–3 embeddable tracks (Spotify/Apple/SoundCloud) + DSP links
4. **Media** — 3–5 hi-res press photos (300dpi), 1–2 live videos, press clips
5. **Stats** — monthly listeners, top markets, notable placements
6. **Live** — upcoming dates, past notable venues/festivals
7. **Contact** — booking + press contacts, socials, website

## One-Sheeter (1 page)
- Photo, 1-sentence bio, 3 bullet stats, 2 track links, booking contact.

## Partnerships One-Pager
- What the artist offers a partner, audience demo, past collabs, contact.

## Bio length cheat-sheet
| Use | Length | Lead with |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| Social bio | 1–2 lines | genre + hook |
| Playlist pitch | 50–75 words | sound + story |
| Press release | 150–200 words | milestone + quote |
| Full EPK | 300–500 words | full arc |
