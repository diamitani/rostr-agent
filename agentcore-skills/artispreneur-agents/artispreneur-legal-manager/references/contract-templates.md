# Contract Template Skeletons

> ⚠️ These are starting points only — NOT legal advice. Always add:
> "This document is informational and does not constitute legal advice.
> Consult a licensed attorney before signing."

## 1. Performance Contract
- Parties (artist / venue / promoter) + legal names
- Date, load-in/soundcheck/set times, venue + address
- Fee + deposit + payment terms (net-30, kill-fee clause)
- Rider (hospitality, technical)
- Cancellation / force majeure
- Signatures + date

## 2. Sync License
- Parties (licensee / licensor)
- Licensed work + ISRC + master vs publishing split
- Media, territory, term, exclusivity
- Fee + royalties
- Credit requirements
- Warranties + signatures

## 3. Collaboration Agreement
- Co-writers + roles (music/lyrics/production)
- Ownership splits (publishing % and master %)
- Attribution + release rights
- Revenue share mechanics
- Signatures (see split-sheet-template.md)

## 4. Management Agreement
- Parties (artist / manager)
- Term + exclusivity + territory
- Commission % + what it covers
- Manager duties vs artist duties
- Expense recoupment
- Termination clause
- Signatures
