# Artist Business Plan Template

> Generated by the Day to Day Manager from the artist's onboarding submission.
> Keep it plain-language — the reader is an indie artist, not an MBA.

## 1. Artist Snapshot
- Name / stage name / genre(s) / primary market
- Career stage: starting | growing | touring | established
- One-line positioning statement

## 2. Current State
- Releases (count, most recent)
- Streaming footprint (monthly listeners, top DSPs)
- Live footprint (shows/year, home market)
- Revenue streams active today

## 3. Goals (12 months)
- 3 measurable goals (e.g. "150K monthly listeners", "20 paid shows", "first sync placement")
- Each with a target date + owner (which sub-agent)

## 4. Gaps & Priorities (NPAO)
| Lens | Finding | Priority |
|------|---------|----------|
| Necessity | must-do now | High/Med/Low |
| Priority | most time-sensitive | ... |
| Anxiety | biggest risk | ... |
| Opportunity | biggest upside | ... |

## 5. Roadmap (see roadmap-template.md)
- Phase 0 PreD → Phase 1 Design → Phase 2 Build → Phase 3 Deploy → Phase 4 Iterate

## 6. Revenue Plan
- Streams to activate (royalties, shows, sync, merch, sponsorships)
- Income goal + milestones (see Finance Manager)

## 7. Team & Tools
- Sub-agents assigned per workstream
- CRM, calendar, email, storage setup status
